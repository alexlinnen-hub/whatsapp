WhatsApp Business setup: Meta steps

Give Focus Group access to your Meta business portfolio, and we'll connect WhatsApp to your AI agent for you. Most customers only need part 3.

Overview

You add our integrations team as an admin on your Meta business portfolio. We then connect your WhatsApp number to your AI agent, so there's nothing technical for you to do.

You

Add Focus Group as an admin (part 3), follow the number setup guide, then send us your number.

Focus Group

Accept your invitation, connect the number to your agent, configure and test it, and look after the connection.

Parts 1 and 2 are optional. Most customers already have a Meta developer account and a business portfolio. If you do, go straight to part 3.

1. Register as a Meta developer Optional

Only needed if you don't already have a Meta developer account. Most customers do, so you can usually skip to part 3.

  1. Open Meta for Developers

    Go to Meta for Developers.

  2. Log in

    Use your Facebook account details.

  3. Register and verify

    Follow the on-screen instructions to register as a developer and verify your account.

  4. Open My Apps

    Once you're registered, click My Apps to confirm you can see the area for creating or managing apps.

You don't need to create an app or add WhatsApp to one. A WhatsApp account set up inside a developer app can't be connected to your AI agent, so please leave that to us.

2. Create a business portfolio Optional

Only needed if you don't already have a Meta business portfolio (formerly called Business Manager). Most customers do, so you can usually skip to part 3.

  1. Open Meta Business Suite

    Go to Meta Business Suite and log in.

  2. Open the business menu

    Click the dropdown menu below Home in the top left corner.

  3. Create a portfolio

    Click Create a business portfolio.

  4. Name it

    Enter a name for your business portfolio. This should be your public business name.

  5. Add your details

    Enter your first and last name and your business email address, then click Create.

  6. Confirm your email

    Check your inbox and confirm your business email address to finish the set-up.

3. Add Focus Group as an admin

This lets our integrations team connect WhatsApp for you, so you never need to share your own login.

  1. Open settings

    Go to Settings in Meta Business Suite.

  2. Open People

    Click People in the left-hand menu.

  3. Invite us

    Click Invite people in the top right.

  4. Enter our email address

    Enter integrations@focusgroup.co.uk

  5. Choose Full control

    Click Next, then select Full control. Meta only lets someone with full control of your portfolio connect WhatsApp to a platform like ours.

  6. Choose assets and send

    Select any business assets you'd like us to manage, such as your WhatsApp account if you already have one, then click Invite.

What happens next

We normally go live within one working day of receiving your details.

  1. Focus Group

    Accept your invitation

    Our integrations team accepts the Meta invitation sent to integrations@focusgroup.co.uk.

  2. Focus Group

    Connect your number

    We connect your WhatsApp number to your AI agent through Meta's official process.

  3. Focus Group

    Configure and test

    We set up how the agent replies, add any integrations, and test messages and voice notes.

  4. Focus Group

    Go live

    We tell you when your agent is live and keep an eye on the connection.

What stays in your control

OwnershipYour business portfolio, WhatsApp account, number and display name stay yours.
BillingMeta bills any WhatsApp charges (such as template messages or outbound calls) directly to the payment method in your WhatsApp Manager. Your Focus Group service covers the AI agent.
Removing our accessYou can remove integrations@focusgroup.co.uk at any time from Settings → People in Meta Business Suite. Please let us know first so we can switch things off cleanly.
